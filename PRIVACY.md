Threadline is a wardrobe app. It is built so that your closet stays on your phone.

## What stays on your device
Your items, their photos, your style profile, your wear history and your reactions to outfit suggestions are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared. Deleting the app deletes them. "Erase all my data" in the Style tab deletes them immediately.

## What leaves your device, and when
- **A garment photo**, once, when you add an item. It is sent to our processing service, which passes it to remove.bg to cut out the background and to Anthropic's Claude API to read what the garment is. The image is not retained by us after the response is returned.
- **A product link**, when you paste or share one, so the page can be read for its name, brand, price and photo.
- **A list of your clean items** (names, categories, colours, formality — no photos), when you ask for an outfit, a wardrobe audit or a packing list, so the suggestion can be generated.
- **Approximate coordinates**, when you allow location, sent to Open-Meteo for the local forecast. They are not stored.

## What we never do
We have no user accounts. We do not sell or share data with advertisers, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use your photos or closet to train models.

## Calendar
If you grant calendar access, event titles and times for the next 36 hours are read on your device to offer occasions. They are not sent anywhere.

## Processors
- Anthropic (Claude API) — garment recognition and outfit reasoning
- remove.bg — background removal
- Open-Meteo — weather forecasts
- Cloudflare — hosting for the processing service

## Children
Threadline is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
