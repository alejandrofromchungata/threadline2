## Name

## Subtitle (iOS, 30 char max)

## Short description (Play, 80 char max)
Photograph your clothes. Get dressed for whatever the day and weather demand.

## Description
Threadline turns the clothes you already own into a closet you can actually use.

Photograph a garment and it is cut off its background, recognised, and filed — category, colour, material, season, formality. Paste or share a product link and the details come across from the retailer's page.

Then ask for an outfit. Threadline picks from what is clean, weighs the forecast where you are, reads what is on your calendar, and learns from every outfit you like or reject.

- Photograph or import your clothes; background removal is automatic
- A searchable, filterable closet with wear counts and cost per wear
- Outfits built for a real occasion, in real weather, from clean clothes only
- Wear log that remembers what you wore and when
- Laundry reminders before the good pieces run out
- Wardrobe audit that names the gaps worth filling
- Packing lists built from the destination forecast

Your closet, photos and history stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

## App Review notes
Threadline has no accounts and no login. To exercise data deletion, open the Style tab and tap "Erase all my data" — this removes everything, since nothing is stored server-side. Camera access is used to photograph clothing; location is coarse and used only for the local forecast; calendar access is read-only and processed on device.
